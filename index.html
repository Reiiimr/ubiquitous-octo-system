Safety net

  • Imports are intercepted: nothing is applied until you choose Replace, Merge, or Cancel.
  • Every import, archive, edit, export, backup and account change is written to the activity log below.
  • Archive instead of delete; archived records can be restored.
  • A restore point is saved before every import; backups are downloaded from Admin settings.

Import / export

Move CSV data in and out of the system safely

Signed in as City Veterinary Office

Before you import: use a CSV (UTF-8, comma-separated) with the exact header row below, dates written YYYY-MM-DD, and barangay names from the official list of 62. Nothing is applied right away: you first see a report of similar records, empty fields and unusual values, then choose Replace, Merge, Inspect anomalies, or Cancel. Paravet census submissions go through the same review (upload as “Census respondents”).
